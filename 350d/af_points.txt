autofocus points selection via
SendToIntercom(7,1,x);

obvious bit pattern is:

00000001 1		center
00000010 2		bottom
00000100 4		top
00001000 8		right
00010000 0x10	left
00100000 0x20	center right
01000000 0x40	center left
10000000 0x80	additional bit



list of tested commands and selected AF points resulting, comments are highlighted with lots of Os not to be overlooked:
