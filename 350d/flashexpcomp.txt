I think the camera does it as follows:
first byte:
0: x=0;
1: x=2;
2: x=4;
3: x=6;
D: x=-6;
E: x=-4;
F: x=-2;

second byte:
0: x=x;
3: x+=0.3;
4: x+=0.5;
5: x+=0.7;
8: x+=1;
b: x+=1.3;
c: x+=1.5;
d: x+=1.7;
