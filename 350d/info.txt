CHARSET

the original menus are using loads of special characters in different languages. didn't yet find out how to use them for the info menu, too.

camera follows ASCII charset from 0x20 to 0x7E

0x00 - 0x1F and 0x7F display blanks

0x80 - 0x88, 0xFF, 0x180, 0x1FF, 0x280: camera crashes.

0x100 - 0x17F, 0x200 - 0x27F : camera behaves like 0x00 - 0x7F
